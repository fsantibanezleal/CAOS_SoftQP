# C11: Conflicting safety boundaries

Category: control. Source: authored synthetic input.

## Why this case exists

Two mutually inconsistent desired boundaries make zero total violation impossible. The optimizer must price the conflict explicitly.

## Formalization

One variable, unit curvature, zero linear target; separate soft rows require x>=1 and x<=-1, with unequal side prices.

Original rows: 2; hard rows: 0; physical units: 1. Row labels and hard/soft policy come from `data-pipeline/pipeline/softqp_rows.py`.

## Expected results

At least one desired row must be violated. A successful elastic optimum is meaningful, whereas claiming feasibility of both original inequalities would be false.
