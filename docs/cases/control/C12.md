# C12: Parameter-stream initial state

Category: control. Source: authored synthetic input.

## Why this case exists

The initial prediction anchors a separate paired parameter stream. All stream methods receive a common independently driven state trajectory.

## Formalization

Initial position 1.6 m; horizon 20; dt=0.2 s; hard acceleration ±1; soft position ±0.4 m and velocity ±1.5 m/s; asymmetric prices.

Original rows: 60; hard rows: 20; physical units: m, m/s, m/s². Row labels and hard/soft policy come from `data-pipeline/pipeline/softqp_rows.py`.

## Expected results

The stream has 32 frames and velocity impulses at frames 8 and 20. Cold/warm setup scopes are separated. Generalized symmetric projection is incompatible for every frame.

## Validation anchor

The independent lifted formulation evaluates the same original objective. Available catalog cells must satisfy normalized objective discrepancy at most 1e-6 and raw hard-row error at most 1e-6. Scalar cases additionally have closed-form checks. Native status, recovered x/y, cost components and lower/upper physical violations remain in the [scientific record](../../../data/derived/softqp/C12.json).

The separate [stream](../../../data/derived/experiments/stream.json) retains every cold/warm frame. It is a shared-trajectory solver comparison, not separate closed-loop controller experiments. Overlapping predicted horizon costs must not be summed as realized cost.
