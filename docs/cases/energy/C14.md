# C14: Side-specific energy trade-offs

Category: energy. Source: OSQPSlack MIT synthetic building model.

## Why this case exists

An authored variant of the building matrices explores side-dependent costs after deliberately replacing the mixed source penalties with separable elastic terms.

## Formalization

The C13 matrices and hard mask are retained. For the separable variant, upper coefficients are three times lower coefficients.

Original rows: 1255; hard rows: 773; physical units: kW, kW/h, kWh, °C. Row labels and hard/soft policy come from `data-pipeline/pipeline/softqp_rows.py`.
