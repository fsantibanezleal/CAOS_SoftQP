# C15: Mixed elastic side costs

Category: energy. Source: OSQPSlack MIT synthetic building model.

## Why this case exists

A second declared separable policy reduces the upper/lower price ratio while retaining the same building structure. It tests policy sensitivity, not a new dataset.

## Formalization

The building matrices and hard mask match C14; the upper coefficient multiplier is 1.5 instead of three.

Original rows: 1255; hard rows: 773; physical units: kW, kW/h, kWh, °C. Row labels and hard/soft policy come from `data-pipeline/pipeline/softqp_rows.py`.

## Expected results

Differences from C14 arise from different prices. They cannot rank numerical accuracy or prove a better energy policy without a practitioner objective and acceptance decision.

## Validation anchor

The independent lifted formulation evaluates the same original objective. Available catalog cells must satisfy normalized objective discrepancy at most 1e-6 and raw hard-row error at most 1e-6. Scalar cases additionally have closed-form checks. Native status, recovered x/y, cost components and lower/upper physical violations remain in the [scientific record](../../../data/derived/softqp/C15.json).
