# C04: Mixed hard and soft rows

Category: integrity. Source: authored synthetic input.

## Why this case exists

Two soft coordinate rows compete with an immutable coupling equality. The case catches implementations that accidentally soften every row.

## Formalization

Two primal variables; coordinate intervals [-1,1]; the hard equality is x1+x2=0. Soft coefficients are asymmetric.

Original rows: 3; hard rows: 1; physical units: 1. Row labels and hard/soft policy come from `data-pipeline/pipeline/softqp_rows.py`.

## Expected results

The equality remains within raw hard tolerance while coordinate violations may be purchased. Objective agreement must preserve the same equality and side costs.
