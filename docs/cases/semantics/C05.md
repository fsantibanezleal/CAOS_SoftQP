# C05: Grouped peak violation

Category: semantics. Source: authored synthetic input.

## Why this case exists

A shared maximum-violation penalty distinguishes a coupled norm from a sum of independent row costs.

## Formalization

Two scalar interval rows share the same infinity-norm penalty group with linear coefficient one.

Original rows: 2; hard rows: 0; physical units: 1. Row labels and hard/soft policy come from `data-pipeline/pipeline/softqp_rows.py`.

## Expected results

The native published group projection and its ordinary lift agree. The separable asymmetric and DAQP adapters cannot replace a grouped maximum with independent prices.
