# Failure-only candidate recovery

Candidate recovery produces a diagnostic readback after the original bake refused, timed out or exceeded a resource cap. It has a separate fixed supervisor route and a separately approved read-only lease. Recovery cannot resume the original clock, launch a candidate worker or authorize a new timed operation. A complete but late prefix is still a failed bake.

## Independent authorization

The recovery authority supplies the SHA-256 of canonical recovery policy bytes outside the submitted files. The policy binds the original request, policy and bake descriptors, original timer bytes and independent boot identifier, independently expected schedule and prospective cohorts, exact frontier bytes and physical journal bytes, and the terminal supervisor receipt and reason. Reference the independently pinned original A/R/P0/P1/T/N/J and selected model/calibration identities. Retain original protocol, registration, source/dependency/producer locks and original I1 evidence as historical provenance. The terminal receipt must agree with the failed operation and prove owned Job closure. A missing supervisor receipt, live Job, valid completed operation, or alternate timer, source, output, roster or journal fails closed.

The new source/profile admission opens an isolated, read-only policy capability for recovery only. Use the exact original committed source and package profile with the recovery route already present in both. Rebind the historical authority bytes as evidence without issuing fresh I1 or enabling the original numerical capability. An excluded replay uses the same independently scoped fixture lineage and still cannot grant I1. Only an exact, bounded, disjoint recovery output root can be created.

## Immutable journal projection

The recovery reader holds the approved original inputs read-only for the recovery lease. Stream the journal and exact scheduled keys independently. Verify every committed prefix byte, predecessor link, next ordinal, exact plan digest, fixed schedule and numerical receipt against the independently expected frontier. Validate any referenced staged terminal bytes for custody, but an uncommitted current intent remains an unobserved interrupted trial even if a staged result exists. Any independently pinned uncommitted suffix is retained as evidence outside committed outcomes. Do not append to the original journal, settle the original frontier, renew the original timer or change the original artifact names.

Independently replay the original read-only candidate evidence validators on committed rows. Check the selected raw SDK result, original-space metrics, exact transformed held-out pair, oracle outcome, frozen model ancestry, original worker startup and observed clock receipt. This path opens the admitted test proof and frozen inference evidence only. It reuses existing published measurements without model deserialization, inference, solver/native trial, label, draw, initializer or data generation. No new numerical worker, fallback, trial nonce, Job-child launch or candidate clock is available.

Project every independently expected status key and its exact warmup/measured phases. Preserve each committed accepted or failed observation and its actual time where independently reverified. Emit the active indeterminate attempt as interrupted with no observation, and all later eligible attempts as missing with the observed termination reason. Preserve incompatible rows with their normative reasons. If recovery encounters tampered or unverifiable evidence, refuse to publish it rather than assign a trial result or conceal a denominator.

## Diagnostic output and resource lease

Publish a dedicated recovery manifest and ordered status/diagnostic streams via the common status reader. Bind original failure, policy, historical timer, expected frontier and journal, the recovered/durable counts, the reported full inventory and actual accepted measurements, and label recovery qualification false in all cases. Deterministic aggregates may recheck already stored data. Validate and quote previously sealed draw bytes only where the corresponding original estimate inputs are complete and the draw reference is independently approved. Missing or incomplete evidence yields undefined hypothesis results and no new bootstrap draws. Preserve the original reasons and keep recovery output distinct from a primary candidate export.

Use one read-only controlled process and the combined 4 GiB working-set bound. The independently expected recovery policy pins a positive administrative lease of at most 24 hours. Start it after supervised source/install admission and before the first recovery evidence read. Check the deadline and a separate 1 GiB cap over staged and final diagnostic files through publication. Expiry leaves no visible recovered result. Require 20 GiB free at startup. Do not allocate or add bytes inside the exhausted candidate benchmark root. Stage into an owned disjoint directory, verify complete raw/canonical readback and publish exclusively. A prepublication rollback leaves the original inputs intact. An approved retry against identical bytes yields identical artifact content.

## Excluded verification

Create an actual owned failure under an independently pinned, prospectively restricted remaining clock and preserve its raw terminal receipt, immutable frontier and cleanup evidence. Exercise both deadline and resource refusal. Recover a late interrupted fixture with committed observations, an unobserved active attempt and missing suffix using the same exact recovery source/profile. Independently compare the complete ordered inventory and original hashes before and after, and observe zero new timed trials, solver calls, model loading and owned numerical child processes. Exercise foreign receipt/scope substitution, active/staged tampering, prior failure preservation, all-recorded late termination, orphaned output and deterministic publication retry. These are software controls.
