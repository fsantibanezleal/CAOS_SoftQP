# Native manuscripts and their evidence

The product has two distinct documents. The technical report explains the entire
implemented tool, its methods, eighteen contracts, pipeline, experiments and
limitations. The research paper derives the side-specific and directional
original-row updates and presents their separate negative timing studies.
Their editable sources are [the technical report](../../manuscripts/soft-constraint-study/main.tex)
and [the research paper](../../manuscripts/coupled-peak-study/main.tex). Both use the
canonical article base, with sections and native mathematical typesetting.

## Write from the implementation and observations

The report follows the problem formulation, notation, data and contracts,
architecture, complete method ladder, protocol, results, validation, discussion,
limitations, reproducibility and conclusions. Every method explains its theory,
equations, parameters, numerical assumptions, algorithm and actual implementation.
Every contract explains its fields, units, producer, consumers, version and missing
data behavior. The schema files remain the machine-readable authority.

The paper adds related work, a derived proposed method, mathematical theory,
prospective hypotheses and experimental design, results with uncertainty, component
comparison, discussion and threats to validity. Closely related methods can share
one paper only when each has its own full derivation and experiment coverage.
Here the two registered studies remain separate populations and analyses within
that paper. A negative result stays visible; neither implementation completion nor
an engineering release establishes timing improvement or priority.

Every float has a descriptive caption of at least fifteen words, a label and an
explicit interpretation in the text. Axes carry their quantities and units.
Figures and numerical tables are generated by committed scripts from declared
artifacts. A figure is evidence for a specific claim, not a decoration or an app
screenshot. Every citation resolves to a primary paper or an official source.
Counts are necessary coverage checks; they never replace reading the scientific
content and checking the derivations.

## Build, inspect and publish

Use the external rendering environment and the commands in
[the manuscript build guide](../../manuscripts/README.md). The build uses native
LaTeX and BibTeX, rejects unresolved references and layout warnings, and verifies
the protected result and figure inputs. Coverage maps bind the implemented methods,
contracts and proposed contributions to real source sections and result figures.
The title and abstract are checked against those results at every new document
version. The current titles explicitly preserve the negative timing findings.

An independent reader reviews the complete content, mathematics and every rendered
page before publication. The publisher requires an explicitly supplied raw review
and an independently trusted digest. A portable historical review summary is
labelled as a display projection and cannot stand in for that raw approval.
Original historical receipts remain preserved; a newly issued portable figure
receipt is a new, limited acceptance of the same unchanged figure evidence.

Each manuscript has its own concept DOI, document type, version and CC BY 4.0
license. A manuscript record contains exactly its PDF; evidence and additional
artifacts belong in separately linked records. Source, staged PDF, metadata, file
set and final public bytes have distinct checks. Method, source-data or scientific
result changes require the corresponding report update. A presentation change
does not silently regenerate a frozen study or turn an old publication approval
into approval of newly changed source.
