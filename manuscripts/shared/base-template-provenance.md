# Base LaTeX identity

Both document types use the article preamble referenced by CAOS_RESEARCH
methodology/05-writing-standards.md:
[the foundational article](https://github.com/fsantibanezleal/CAOS_RESEARCH/blob/main/manuscripts/jacobian-conjecture/foundational/main.tex) in CAOS_RESEARCH.
The original is preserved in the private revision evidence. The mathematical
macros specific to the Jacobian problem are replaced with the notation of convex
optimization; its article class, 1.1-inch margin, section-numbered theorem family,
navy links, ORCID, programme affiliation, boxed identity and running headers remain.
Additional packages support native algorithms, vector figures and contract tables.

The product report and the scientific paper have different document-type labels,
titles, coverage maps and concept identifiers. Neither contains chapter commands.
Class alone does not establish document type or scientific content. Current content,
source, mathematics and complete rendered-page acceptance are required separately.
