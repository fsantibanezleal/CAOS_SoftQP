\section{Complete original timing cells}\label{sec:timing-appendix}

Table~\ref{tab:timing-cells} expands the original aggregate timing summary
used by the experiment reader. Its 600 rows retain 288 development cells,
288 held-out cells and 24 robustness cells. A row describes one method
at a specified split, penalty family, decision dimension $n$, matrix
density and diagonal Hessian condition $\kappa$. The split codes
D, H and R denote development, held-out and robustness. The family codes
AE, AL and SE denote asymmetric elastic, asymmetric linear and symmetric
elastic penalties. Gen.\ projection identifies the published generalized
slack projection. Side-specific identifies the original-row separable
interval update.

Each populated timing field gives the median followed by the first and
third quartiles of cold validated-call durations, in milliseconds.
The A and I counts preserve the accepted and incompatible measured
observations. There were no failed, rejected or timed-out observations in
this original grid. An incompatible cell has no duration. Each cell
combines repetitions and seeds at its own parameter settings, so its
interquartile range describes dispersion within that cell. The 95\%
seed-cluster interval for the paired primary endpoint instead belongs to
Table~\ref{tab:main-results}. The two summaries answer different questions.
The complete cell inventory lets a reader inspect the same filters as
the browser without treating a single selected slice as the primary
analysis.
