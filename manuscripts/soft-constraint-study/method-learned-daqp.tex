\subsection{Graph-predicted working sets for DAQP}\label{sec:method-learned-daqp}
Schmidtobreick, Arnstr\"om, H\"ausner and Sj\"olund use a graph model
to initialize a dual active-set solver
(\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13174v2}{primary source}).
The SoftQP adapter uses this approach with DAQP \cite{daqp} on a
supported positive-definite convex lift. A backend curvature or
numerical refusal is retained. The graph does not change the quadratic
objective or the feasible set.

Let \(P,q,G,b\) denote the admitted dimensionless lifted Hessian,
linear cost, one-sided matrix and right-hand side. The row map
contains each equality once and otherwise lists finite upper and
lower sides in that order, with signs \(+1\) and \(-1\). There is
one variable node per lifted coordinate and one constraint node per
retained side or equality. The four feature coordinates are
\((q_j,0,0,0)\) for variable \(j\) and
\((0,b_i,\mathbf1_{\mathrm{eq},i},1)\) for row \(i\).
Each graph standardizes each coordinate across its own nodes with
population variance and unit scale at zero variance. Nonzero
\(P_{jk}\) entries supply signed variable-to-variable edges, and
each nonzero \(G_{ij}\) supplies a signed edge in both directions
between variable \(j\) and constraint \(i\). The constraint-variable
incidence is bipartite; the additional Hessian edges convey
interactions among variables.

Three weighted local extrema convolution layers have widths
\(4\to128\to128\to1\). The first two are followed by layer normalization
and leaky rectification with slope \(0.1\). There are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:learn-daqp-parameters}
 \sum_{(a,b)\in\{(4,128),(128,128),(128,1)\}}(3ab+2b)+4(128)
 =52\,098
\end{equation}
trainable scalars. Each convolution has three matrices and two
biases. The two normalization layers each contribute 128 weights
and 128 biases. The final scalar logit \(g_i\) gives
\(p_i=(1+\exp(-g_i))^{-1}\).

Three independently evaluated reference solves must agree before a
fresh cold DAQP solve is eligible to supply labels. Its initial flags
contain only the immutable equalities. After an acceptable DAQP
status and the tight lifted and original-space checks, take both
the primal vector \(z\) and the unmodified raw one-sided dual
\(\lambda_i\) from that same cold solve. Put \(s_i=b_i-G_i z\) and
\(\tau_i=10^{-7}\max(1,|b_i|)\). A positive target has
\(|s_i|\le\tau_i\) and \(\lambda_i>10^{-7}\); a negative target
has \(s_i>\tau_i\) and \(|\lambda_i|\le10^{-7}\). Ambiguous rows,
equality rows and variable nodes carry no supervised target.
Let \(M\) be the retained labels and \(m_c\) the train-only,
source-weighted mass of class \(c\). The loss per nonempty frame is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:learn-daqp-bce}
 \mathcal L=\frac{1}{|M|}\sum_{i\in M}\frac{m_0+m_1}{2m_{t_i}}
       \log(1+\exp((1-2t_i)g_i)).
\end{equation}
Here \(t_i\in\{0,1\}\) is the admitted binary label. An empty mask
contributes zero gradient, while missing class mass prevents model
qualification. The fit uses three seeds 41001--41003 and AdamW with
eight sources per batch, rate \(10^{-3}\), decay \(10^{-2}\),
at most 100 epochs, patience five and minimum improvement \(10^{-3}\).
A separate calibration role selects from thresholds 0.1 through 0.9
by source-level macro F1, preferring the larger threshold on exact
ties. Calibration is undefined when every eligible calibration mask
is empty. Individual empty-mask frames are retained. An unavailable
calibration prevents a qualified learned solve.

\begin{algorithm}[ht]
\caption{Predict, repair and verify a DAQP working set}\label{alg:learn-daqp}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State Verify the model, lift and frozen threshold and compute node probabilities.
\State Mark inequalities active for \(p_i>\theta\) and keep equalities immutable.
\State Attempt DAQP with the proposed flags.
\While{the native status is \(-6\) and a predicted inequality remains}
 \State Remove the lowest-confidence active prediction and retry.
\EndWhile
\State Retain each attempt and check the recovered candidate independently.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

Removal breaks confidence ties by original interval row and then
one-sided position. The last permitted retry may have no
predicted inequalities. Other failure codes do not trigger arbitrary
repair. The matched DAQP 0.10.3 routes pass primal and dual tolerances
\(10^{-7}\), zero threshold \(10^{-12}\), limit \(200\,000\),
zero proximal correction and \texttt{eq\_reduction=-1}.
The cold control starts with equalities alone; previous initialization
uses only an accepted compatible preceding state with admitted
multiplier and tightness evidence. For this trio of one-shot routes,
the reported argument values are those passed to the native call.
The thin legacy elastic route separately supports native-instance
settings observations before and after its solve. The primary
measurement includes graph feature construction, inference, setup,
all repair attempts and the final checks. Inference cost depends on
the number of graph nodes and signed edges, and solver cost on the
working-set linear algebra and the number of retries. Status 1 or 2
is required but still subject to independent acceptance. Signed
one-sided multipliers accumulate with their side signs to recover
the interval dual before original-space evaluation.
